About.

A studio built on the belief that great work comes from people who care about every line, every detail, and every client.

Our studio

A BC-based design & drafting studio.

"Where structure meets style, and every line tells a story."

Rexford is a British Columbia-based design and drafting studio known for its thoughtful approach to capturing the character of every space. Our team brings together architectural precision and a genuine eye for design, guided by clarity, craftsmanship, and care.

From the first concept sketch through permit-ready drawings and interior selections, we translate ideas into architectural form with precision and purpose. We work with homeowners, developers, and builders across BC and Alberta, providing drawings and design services that integrate with any project team. For clients who need it, we also offer construction management and project oversight to see the work through to completion.

We're based in Chilliwack, BC, and work across British Columbia and Alberta.
