How we work

From first conversation
to key handover.

Every project starts with a proper design phase, with each detail resolved on paper before site work begins. When we're managing construction too, that same care carries through to the finished space.

01

Discovery

We learn your project, including site conditions, program, budget, and timeline. No assumptions before we understand the brief.

02

Design

Concept drawings, permit documents, finish schedules, and specifications. The studio works until every detail is resolved on paper.

03

Deliver

Permit drawings, interior specifications, and full documentation ready for your build team, or ours if you need site management.

04

Handover

A proper walkthrough, all your documentation, and follow-up once you're settled in. We don't call it done until it is.

Why Rexford

One firm. Full accountability.

Integrated under one roof

Design and construction handled by the same firm, under one contract. No miscommunication between the designer and the builder, because they're the same people.

Schedule discipline

We set a construction timeline before breaking ground and hold it. Our trade coordination process is built around the schedule, not around waiting.

Drawn, then built

Every finish, fitting, and detail is specified on paper before construction starts. The design studio resolves the hard decisions up front, so the site team isn't making them on the fly.
