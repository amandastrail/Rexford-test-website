A design and drafting studio in Chilliwack, BC.

Working with homeowners, developers, and builders across British Columbia and Alberta.

People who care about every line.

Rexford is a British Columbia design and drafting studio known for a thoughtful approach to the character of every space. The team brings together architectural precision and a genuine eye for design.

From the first concept sketch through permit-ready drawings and interior selections, we translate ideas into architectural form. Our drawings integrate with any project team, and for clients who want it, we also offer construction management to see the work through.
