Accounting & Bookkeeping

Jacinta Hunt

Construction-industry financial specialist and founder of Destiny Bookkeeping, translating complex financial data into actionable intelligence for the Rexford team.

Background

Jacinta Hunt is the accounting and bookkeeping lead at Rexford Drafting & Design Inc., bringing extensive experience in financial management. She is the founder of Destiny Bookkeeping and has developed specialized expertise in construction-industry finances through hands-on work across construction, trades, and project-based firms.

Her expertise spans job costing and cash flow management, financial systems design and budgeting frameworks, cost-control strategies for contractors and designers, compliance, invoicing, and project profitability monitoring.

Role at Rexford

Jacinta serves as the financial anchor at Rexford, translating complex financial data into actionable business intelligence. She enables leadership to focus on design and delivery while maintaining financial clarity, ensuring compliance and providing insights that drive growth and scaling confidence.

Her commitment centres on building internal and client relationships while delivering industry-specific knowledge that supports organizational health and sustainable expansion. Every project is seen through to financial completion with the same attention to detail she brings to the books.

"Financial clarity gives a studio the freedom to do its best work, and when the numbers are right, everyone can focus on what they do best."

Jacinta Hunt, Accounting Lead
