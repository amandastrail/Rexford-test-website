Background

Andrew joined the Rexford construction team in 2020 and has become a key contributor on the job site. He is recognized for his precision and strong work ethic, with a particular focus on quality and accuracy that ensures projects meet the highest standards regardless of complexity.

His attention to detail isn't limited to professional projects. In his spare time, Andrew built his own home, applying the same level of excellence he brings to every Rexford build, and demonstrating what it looks like to truly care about the craft.

What drives him

Andrew demonstrates a genuine love of craftsmanship and is driven by curiosity about the building industry. He continuously expands his skill set by learning new trade techniques and actively pursuing hands-on learning, the kind of builder who asks why, not just how.

He thrives on new challenges and approaches every endeavour, whether professional or personal, with a commitment to quality that's hard to miss when you see the finished product.

"Every detail matters. You can see it in the finished work, or you can see where it was skipped."

— Andrew Wiebe, Construction Team
