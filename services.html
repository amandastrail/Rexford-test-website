What we offer

Services.

Architecture, interiors, drafting, and styling, with project management and construction oversight when you need it.

Where creativity meets craftsmanship

Design-led, start to finish.

Most studios do one thing well. We do all four, and because they're led by the same team, they work together. Design decisions inform construction budgets. Construction realities shape design choices. The result is a project that arrives at completion the way it was conceived.

01

Drafting & Design

Architectural Services

We provide detailed architectural drafting and design services for residential and commercial projects of all sizes. Our drawings are used by developers, builders, and contractors across BC and Alberta, and we work alongside any build team. Every line is drawn to be built, not just presented.

  • Concept design & schematic drawings
  • Permit-ready construction documents
  • Site plans, elevations & sections
  • 3D visualization & renderings
  • As-built drawings & documentation
  • Residential & multi-family projects

Interior Design

Full-Service Interiors

We craft interiors that balance beauty and purpose. We work with homeowners, developers, and builders at any stage of a project, from early material selections alongside architectural plans to finished-space styling. Timeless aesthetics and practical details that hold up over time.

  • Space planning & layout
  • Material & finish selection
  • Custom millwork design
  • Lighting design & specification
  • Furniture selection & sourcing
  • Full home & commercial interiors

Interior Styling

Finishing & Styling

The final layer that brings a space to life. Interior styling covers the finishing details, including artwork, soft furnishings, and the careful placement of elements that make a house feel like a home or a commercial space feel considered and complete.

  • Move-in styling & installation
  • Artwork curation & placement
  • Soft furnishing selection

Why it works

Design leads the work.

We resolve every finish, fitting, and specification on paper before anything is ordered or installed. The design phase is where decisions get made thoughtfully, not reactively.
