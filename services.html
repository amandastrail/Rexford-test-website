Common questions

Questions we get asked.

What services does Rexford offer?

Site planning, architectural drafting, custom homes, renovations, multi-family, commercial, and interior design. Design and drafting are handled by one team, under one roof. For clients who would rather have a single firm carry the project through construction, we also offer construction management.

Do you handle both design and drafting, or do you hand off to a separate drafter?

We handle both. It is worth asking any firm this directly, because the answer tells you a lot about how your project will run. When design and drafting are split between two firms, the drafter does not know why each decision was made, so practical changes get resolved structurally rather than in service of the design. Revision cycles multiply.

With one team, design intent carries through to the construction documents, and you have one point of contact throughout. More on design versus drafting.

How long does it take to build a custom home in BC?

Typically between 18 months and 3 years, from the first design conversation to moving in. Design and drawings account for roughly three to six months, permit review for one to four months depending on the municipality, and construction for the balance.

The design phase is where most timeline surprises happen. See the full phase-by-phase breakdown.

What does working with Rexford actually look like?

Four steps. First, an initial inquiry: tell us about the project, the site, and the timeline, and we confirm fit and send a proposal within two business days. Second, concept and schematic design, developing layout and massing against your brief, the site, and local zoning. Third, design development, where details, specifications, and coordination across every sheet get resolved. Fourth, the permit-ready set, submitted to your municipality, with us handling any revisions the authority requires.

Who do you work with?

Homeowners building or renovating, builders and developers managing multiple sites or a subdivision, and contractors whose client has a design but no drawings. We integrate with existing build teams and work to their schedules.

Our studio is in Chilliwack, British Columbia, and we work across BC and Alberta. You can reach us at 778.808.6397 or info@rexfordco.ca.
