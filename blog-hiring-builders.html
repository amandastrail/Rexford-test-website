Essential Steps for Hiring Builders and Designers When Building Your New Home

Amanda Wiebe 3 min read

Building a new home is an exciting journey, but it can also feel overwhelming. One of the biggest challenges is finding the right homebuilder and designer. Selecting the right team is crucial to avoiding delays, unexpected costs, and a final result that doesn’t match your expectations.

Understand Your Vision and Budget

Before contacting professionals, clarify your home requirements, including the style and size of the house, the key features and rooms you need, any sustainability or energy efficiency goals, and your overall budget range. Having clear priorities enables effective communication and realistic planning.

Research and Shortlist Qualified Builders and Designers

Gather recommendations from friends, family, or local homebuilding associations. Evaluate candidates based on their experience with similar new builds, proper licensing and insurance, positive client testimonials, and transparent communication. Create a shortlist of at least three professionals to interview.

Interview and Ask the Right Questions

Conduct face-to-face or video meetings with potential candidates. Come prepared to discuss:

  • Their experience with projects similar to yours
  • How they handle permits and inspections
  • Timeline estimates and how they manage delays
  • Communication methods and frequency of updates
  • How they handle changes or unexpected costs
  • References from recently completed projects

Pay attention to how clearly they explain things and their willingness to collaborate.

Review Contracts and Understand Terms

Examine contracts carefully. Look for a detailed scope of work and materials list, the payment schedule and total cost breakdown, warranty and post-construction support terms, procedures for change orders or disputes, and timeline milestones with any delay penalty provisions. Seek clarification on anything unclear, or consult a construction lawyer before signing.

Collaborate on Design and Planning

Work with your designer to finalize the plans, reviewing sketches, selecting materials and finishes, and coordinating construction feasibility with the builder. This phase also covers obtaining permit-ready drawings and approvals. Strong communication among all parties establishes the foundation for a successful build.

Monitor Construction Progress

Stay involved throughout. Visit the site regularly, request updates and photos when you can’t visit in person, address concerns immediately with your builder, and track how the budget and timeline are trending. Early awareness of issues is far cheaper than late discoveries.

Prepare for Move-In and Final Inspections

As completion approaches, conduct a thorough walkthrough with both the builder and designer. Document any defects or unfinished work, ensure all permits and occupancy certificates are finalized, and plan your move-in logistics. A final inspection confirms the build meets quality standards before you take possession.
