What we do

Design.
Plan.
Create.

Rexford is a design and drafting studio based in Chilliwack, BC. We produce architectural drawings and interior designs that work alongside any build team. For clients who want one firm handling both, we offer construction management too.

We resolve every finish, material, and detail on paper before construction begins. That process is what keeps projects on track and finished spaces looking the way they were designed.
