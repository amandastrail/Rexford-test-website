Process.

A design-led process. We resolve your project completely on paper, hand you a package any builder can build from, and stay accountable to the design every step after.

How we work

The drawings decide everything.

Every successful build is decided long before anyone breaks ground. The drawings determine what the project costs, how smoothly it permits, and whether the finished space feels the way you imagined it.

That is why Rexford is design-led. We are a drafting and design studio first: our process is built to resolve every decision on paper and produce documents any builder can price and build from. If you want one team to carry the project through construction as well, we offer construction management as an add-on. It is an option, never a requirement.

01
Phase One

Discovery & Brief

Before anything is drawn, we spend time understanding your project. Site conditions, program requirements, budget parameters, timeline expectations, and how you intend to use the space, and all of it matters. No assumptions before we understand the brief.

What happens

  • Initial consultation
  • Site visit & assessment
  • Program development
  • Budget framing
  • Feasibility review

What you receive

  • Project brief document
  • Preliminary budget range
  • Timeline overview
  • Go/no-go decision
02
Phase Two

Concept & Design

This is where the project takes shape. The studio explores concept options with you, then iterates through schematic drawings and design development until the plan is right. Interior design runs alongside the architecture, so finishes, fixtures, and millwork are decided with the spaces rather than after them.

What happens

  • Concept design
  • Schematic drawings
  • Design development
  • Interior design direction
  • Client reviews & revisions

What you receive

  • Concept & schematic drawings
  • Developed plans & elevations
  • Preliminary finish direction
  • Updated budget alignment
03
Phase Three

Permit & Construction Documents

Design becomes documentation. We produce permit-ready drawings and a complete construction package: specifications, finish schedules, and millwork drawings, with engineering coordinated along the way. Nothing is left for a site crew to guess at, and that completeness is what protects your budget.

What happens

  • Permit drawings & application
  • Engineering coordination
  • Construction documents
  • Finish schedule & specifications
  • Millwork drawings

What you receive

  • Permit-ready drawing set
  • Specifications package
  • Finish schedule
  • Millwork drawings
  • Updated budget
04
Phase Four

Builder Handoff & Design Support

With the documents complete, you can take the package to any builder, or one we help you select. We stay available through construction to answer site questions, review shop drawings, and make sure what gets built matches what was drawn. The design stays accountable to the end.

What happens

  • Builder selection support
  • Pricing & tender questions
  • Shop drawing review
  • Site questions & clarifications
  • Design-intent reviews

What you receive

  • A builder-ready package
  • Ongoing design support
  • A build that matches the drawings
Optional add-on

Want one team end to end? Add construction management.

Some clients take our drawings to their own builder. Others want the team that designed the project to see it built. Through our construction division, Rexford offers construction management as an add-on to any design engagement.

The advantage is continuity: the people running your site know the intent behind every line on the drawings, so nothing gets lost in translation between the studio and the field.

Before your first conversation

What's helpful to have ready.

Site or address

Whether you own the lot already or are still looking, knowing the location helps us understand zoning, setbacks, and site conditions before we meet.

A rough budget range

You don't need a number to the dollar. A range tells us whether we can design the project you have in mind, and helps us prioritize where the money should go.

A sense of what you want

Photos, a list, a description, or anything that communicates how you want to live in the space. The more we understand your intentions, the better the brief we can write.
