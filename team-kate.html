Background

Kate is a Junior Architectural Draftsperson who supports the Rexford team in the development of residential drawings, project documentation, and interior design selections. Having recently completed the Architectural Drafting Technician program at the University of the Fraser Valley, she is proficient in Revit, AutoCAD, and SketchUp, with a strong understanding of the BC Building Code.

Role at Rexford

With a keen eye for detail, a creative approach to design, and a passion for creating functional, beautiful spaces, Kate enjoys contributing to both the architectural and interior design aspects of each project while continuing to grow her experience and deliver thoughtful, high-quality work.
