Interior Designer

Sarah Penner

Specializing in kitchen and bath design, creating functional, beautiful spaces that truly reflect how each client lives in their home.

Background

Sarah is a recent graduate of BCIT's Residential Interiors Diploma program, bringing fresh academic training to an experienced team. She joined Rexford as Junior Interior Designer and has quickly become a trusted contributor to the studio's design process.

Her specialization in kitchen and bath design gives her a sharp eye for the spaces where function and beauty must co-exist most deliberately, where everyday life is lived most closely with the design decisions made.

Philosophy

Sarah embraces a collaborative design process and draws inspiration from client conversations. She combines creativity with a personable approach, prioritizing an understanding of each client's needs above all else.

She thrives on exploring creative possibilities alongside clients, not imposing a style, but drawing out the spaces that feel genuinely theirs. Every project is a chance to translate someone's lifestyle into something tangible and lasting.

"Creating functional, beautiful spaces that reflect each client's unique lifestyle, and that's what good interior design does."

Sarah Penner, Interior Designer
