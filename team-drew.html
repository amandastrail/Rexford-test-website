Drew

27+ years of drafting and design experience across BC, Alberta, and Saskatchewan, with technical precision grounded in real-world construction insight.

Background

Originally from Australia, Drew grew up immersed in construction through his father's work. After moving to Canada in 1993, he continued building practical knowledge by working summer projects in Chilliwack, BC, before pursuing formal training in Architectural Drafting and Design at the University of the Fraser Valley.

With over 27 years of professional experience in architectural drafting and design, plus a decade as a structural drafter for a leading BC structural engineering firm, Drew's portfolio spans residential, commercial, and multi-family developments across BC, Alberta, and Saskatchewan.

Beyond the drafting table

Drew has volunteered his design expertise for meaningful humanitarian causes, including designing a school in Mirebalais, Haiti following the 2010 earthquake. That same commitment to purposeful design informs everything he brings to Rexford projects.

His work is characterized by a commitment to craftsmanship, collaboration, and meaningful design, bringing a rare combination of technical precision and real-world insight to every project.

"Technical precision and real-world insight aren't opposites, and the best drawings come from understanding both sides of the wall."

— Drew Delbridge, Senior Designer
